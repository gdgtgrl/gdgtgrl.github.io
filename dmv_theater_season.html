Milestone Anniversaries: The Heartbeat of the DMV

The 2026-2027 calendar is defined by historic longevity. Two of the region's most prominent producing organizations are celebrating massive milestones, proving the resilience of the local arts ecosystem. These anniversaries are being marked by massive community initiatives and premiere-heavy programming.

75

Arena Stage

Diamond Anniversary

Celebrating 75 years as a pioneer of the regional theater movement. Under Artistic Director Hana S. Sharif, their season features a massive New Play Festival, a revival of Damn Yankees, and the world premiere of Fremont Ave.

50

Round House Theatre

Golden Anniversary

Marking 50 years since their 1977 debut, Round House is launching the "50 for 50" initiative—providing 50 free theatrical experiences to the community alongside major mainstage hits like August Wilson's Ma Rainey's Black Bottom.
